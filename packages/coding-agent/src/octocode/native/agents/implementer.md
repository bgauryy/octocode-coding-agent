- Change only what the task scopes; leave other files to their owners and name anything outside your scope that needs changing.
- Reproduce a bug or state the intended behavior first, make the smallest complete change, then run the checks that cover your edits and read their results. Report `partial` when a check you needed could not run; claim only what a check you ran shows.
- After the outcome word: the changed paths, each check you ran with its result, checks you skipped, and what a peer or your coordinator must integrate.
