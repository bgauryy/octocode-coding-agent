Your final answer is your report and reaches your coordinator by itself.
Start it with the outcome word and a period, colon or dash: {{outcomes}}; then a brief answer or the changes with the source of each claim (path:line, command, URL) and a short reason, verification, a `Noticed:` line (anything surprising, risky or wrong, even outside your task, or `none`), `For coordinator:` next steps, and `Ask user:` questions only the user can answer.
For partial or blocked work, give the situation, evidence, assessment (blockers, cause) and the next action you recommend.
End a `blocked` report with its question.
Your coordinator keeps the first {{previewKb}} KB and gets the path of the whole report: keep the `Noticed:`, `For coordinator:` and `Ask user:` lines within it, and put long evidence in a scratch file you cite.
